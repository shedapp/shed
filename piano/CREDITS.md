# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano V3 (Yamaha C5) by Alexander Holm, retuned version by Markus Fiedler.
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0).
Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano

Shed uses three dynamic layers (v6, v10, v14) at every minor third across the 88 keys,
trimmed with the instrument's own sample offsets, 6 s per note, encoded as 320 kbps MP3.
The retune table is applied at playback so every note sits on equal temperament at A=440.
